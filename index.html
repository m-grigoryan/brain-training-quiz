Math Practice

Focus on addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division. Every correct answer makes that operation more challenging.

Select one or more operations

+ and − start with 4-digit numbers.

× and ÷ start with 1-digit numbers.

Every correct answer adds one digit for that operation, up to 32 digits. Incorrect answers keep the same level. Division is always exact; the dividend grows each level and the divisor grows every second level.

5 questions per session, with no timer. Math sessions and answers are saved in their own history.

Number Flow

Keep a running total as one operation appears every three seconds. Five short rounds. One final answer each.

Choose your operations
